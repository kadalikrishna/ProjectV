About HDI

A simple score for development progress

The Human Development Index is a number between 0 and 1 that summarizes how well a country is doing in long life, learning, and standard of living.

Key Factors That Shape HDI

Four important indicators used to estimate human development.

Health

Life Expectancy

A longer life expectancy indicates better health outcomes.

Education

Schooling Years

Mean and expected schooling reflect current and future learning.

Income

GNI Per Capita

Income per person reflects economic strength and living standards.
